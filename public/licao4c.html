💡 Grammar Tip
📚 Have got / Has got
Use have got / has got for possession and relationships:

• Affirmative (+):
I / You / We / They have got ('ve got) a map.
He / She / It has got ('s got) a smartphone.

• Negative (-):
I / You / We / They haven't got GPS.
He / She / It hasn't got battery.

• Questions (?):
Have you got a ticket?
Has she got a street map?

• Short Answers:
Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.
Yes, she has. / No, she hasn't.
(Note: Never say Yes, I got or No, I haven't got).
