Invitations & Preferences:

"Would you like to + base verb" is a polite and useful invitation pattern:
e.g. Would you like to have dinner tonight?

To ask about preferences, use "Do you like...?":
e.g. Do you like spicy Mexican food?

After like / love / hate, the -ing form is common:
e.g. I love travelling and eating out.
