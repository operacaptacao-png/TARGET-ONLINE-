Language Guide

What to wear when it's cold?
This is for general situations, like when you're talking about clothing for cold days in general.

What to wear in summer?
This is also for general situations, such as discussing what to wear during the summer season.

What are you wearing?
This is for now, when you want to know what someone is wearing at this moment.
