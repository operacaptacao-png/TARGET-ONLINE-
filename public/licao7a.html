Present Continuous Tense:

The Present Continuous describes actions happening right now at this moment:
subject + am/is/are + verb-ing

Examples:
• She is listening to music.
• They are talking about the flat.
• He is eating delicious pizza.

Negatives use not (is not / isn't, are not / aren't) and questions invert (Are you studying?).
