Present Continuous (Actions happening now)

Structure: Subject + BE (am / is / are) + Verb-ing

• I am cooking dinner.
• He / She is reading a book.
• They are talking on Queen’s Road.

Negatives: is not (isn't) / are not (aren't) + verb-ing.
Questions: Are you listening?
