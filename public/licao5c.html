BE vs. HAVE GOT & Demonstratives

1. BE (Physical Attributes & Age):

Use am / is / are for stature, general body type, and age:
• He is tall, slim, and young.
• She is medium-height and athletic.

2. HAVE GOT (Features & Possessions):

Use have got / has got for hair, eyes, facial features:
• He has got short black hair and blue eyes.
• They have got glasses and curly hair.

3. Demonstratives (Position & Distance):

• THIS (singular, near): This is Alan right beside me.
• THAT (singular, far): That is Samuel across the room.
• THESE (plural, near): These are my notes right here.
• THOSE (plural, far): Those are our colleagues by the window.
