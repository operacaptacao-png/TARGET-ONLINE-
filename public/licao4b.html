2. Target Situation • Asking for the British Museum

Ivete has stepped off the bus and needs directions to reach the British Museum entrance.

Ivete: "Excuse me! Could you tell me the way to the British Museum, please?"
Local Pedestrian: "Yes, of course! Go straight on down this avenue until you see the traffic lights."
Ivete: "And what do I do at the traffic lights?"
Local Pedestrian: "Turn right into Great Russell Street. Walk past the bookshop, and the museum will be directly on your left."
Ivete: "Is it far from here?"
Local Pedestrian: "Not at all, only about five minutes on foot. You can't miss it!"

1. What should Ivete do at the traffic lights?

2. Where is the British Museum located?

💡 Grammar Tip
📚 Giving Directions (Imperatives)
Use the base form of the verb for direct instructions:
• Go straight on (Siga em frente)
• Turn left / right (Vire à esquerda / direita)
• Take the second turning (Pegue a segunda entrada)
• Cross the street (Atravesse a rua)
• It's on your left / right (Fica à sua esquerda / direita)
