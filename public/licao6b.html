Adverbs of Frequency:

Adverbs of frequency usually come before the main verb:
e.g. I always go to the gym. We usually watch movies.

With the verb BE, they come after:
e.g. I am always tired after work.

To ask about frequency, use "How often...?":
e.g. How often do you travel?
