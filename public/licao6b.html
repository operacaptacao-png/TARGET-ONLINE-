Advérbios de Frequência & Restaurantes

1. Posição dos Advérbios:

• Antes de verbos principais: I usually order pasta.
• Depois do verbo BE: They are always happy together.

2. Escala de Frequência:

100% Always ➔ Usually ➔ Often ➔ Sometimes ➔ Hardly ever / Rarely ➔ 0% Never.

3. Pedindo no Restaurante:

• Could we have the menu, please?
• For my main course, I'd like the grilled salmon.
• Could we get the bill, please?
