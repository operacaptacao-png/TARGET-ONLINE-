Language Guide

We use "CAN I HAVE...?" and "I’D LIKE..." to ask for things.

💡 GRAMMAR TIP
We use would like in offers and requests. (I'd like = I would like)

• Would you like anything to drink?
• I would like a large soda, please.
1. I a cup of coffee, please.
2. you like to order?
3. I orange juice, please.
