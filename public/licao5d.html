Unit 5 Master Reference

1. CAN / CAN'T (Abilities)

No 'to' after can! I can speak English. / He can't code.

2. Adverbs of Frequency

Position: Before main verbs, after verb TO BE. I always arrive early. / She is usually happy.

3. BE vs. HAVE GOT

BE: Height, build, age (He is tall/young).
HAVE GOT: Eyes, hair, features (She has got curly hair).

4. Demonstratives

This (near, sing.) | These (near, plur.)
That (far, sing.) | Those (far, plur.)
