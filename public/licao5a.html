💡 Grammar Tip
📚 Modal Verb: Can / Can't
Use can / can't for abilities and skills:

• Affirmative (+):
I can manage groups.
She can type fast.
⚠️ RULE: Never use "to" after can! (Say: I can drive, never I can to drive).

• Negative (-):
I can't (cannot) dance.
He can't speak Spanish.
(Never say I don't can or She not can).

• Questions (?):
Can you speak English?
What sports can you play?

• Short Answers:
Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
