Unit 6 Key Grammar

• Past of Regular Verbs: add -ed (talked, loved, ordered).
• Irregular Past: eat ➔ ate, drink ➔ drank, take ➔ took.
• Past Negatives: didn't + base verb (didn't talk, didn't enjoy).
• Past Questions: Did + subject + base verb? (Did he call her?)
