1. Words and Ideas • London Transit & Signs

Listen to the transit terms, then drag each sign to its matching definition or street situation.

Track 4A.1: Public Transport & Traffic Signs
A. Bus Passenger Waiting Point

A designated shelter or pole along the street where commuters queue to board.

B. Exclusive Transport Lane

Marked lane on the avenue reserved specifically for public double-decker buses.

C. Prohibited Street Access

Red circular symbol warning vehicles they cannot enter this one-way road.

D. Compulsory Vehicle Halt

Octagonal red sign demanding full halt before proceeding at junctions.

E. Pedestrian Road Crossing

Striped street zone where people walking always have legal priority over traffic.

F. Circular Traffic Junction

Round road junction where cars travel clockwise around a central island.

G. Subterranean Rail Network

London's famous iconic subway line for rapid transit across boroughs.

H. Fare Payment / Oyster Reader

Electronic point where commuters tap contactless cards or recharge travel passes.

2. Target Situation • Boarding Route 14

Ivete is at Piccadilly Circus and needs to catch a red bus to Oxford Street. Listen to the exchange with the driver.

Track 4A.2: Ivete & London Bus Driver
🚍 London Bus Line: Route 14 Destination: Oxford Circus via Piccadilly
Driver London Bus Route 14
💬
Boarding Conversation
Ivete Passenger
Driver: "Morning! Where are you heading today, love?"
Ivete: "Good morning! Does this bus go to Oxford Street?"
Driver: "Yes, it does! Straight along Regent Street to Oxford Circus."
Ivete: "Wonderful. How much is the fare, please?"
Driver: "It's £1.75. Just tap your Oyster or contactless card on the yellow reader. Remember, we don't accept cash on London buses!"
Ivete: "Understood. Could you please let me know when we arrive at my stop?"
Driver: "Sure thing. It's about five stops ahead. Take a seat upstairs if you want the best view!"
READY TO TAP: FARE £1.75
