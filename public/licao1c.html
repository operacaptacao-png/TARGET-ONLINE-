Target Situation

Listen to the dialogue

Read the dialogue between Ivete and the hotel's receptionist, then answer the questions.

Ivete: Good morning. My name is Ivete Alves. I have a booking here for 5 nights.
Receptionist: Wait a minute, please… Could you spell your name, please?
Ivete: I-V-E-T-E A-L-V-E-S
Receptionist: Thank you! … Here it is. Room 1029 for 5 nights.
Ivete: That’s correct.
Receptionist: May I See your passport, please?
Ivete: Here it is.
Receptionist: Thank you. Can you fill in this registration card, please?
Ivete: Sure thing!... Could I use your pen?
Receptionist: Sure!
Ivete: Here it is. Thank you very much.
Receptionist: You’re welcome! This is your key! Room number 1029.
Ivete: Lovely! Thank you!
a. What does the receptionist say to greet Ivete?
b. How many nights does Ivete have a booking for?
c. Why does the receptionist ask Ivete to spell her name?
d. What is the room number assigned to Ivete?
e. What does the receptionist request to see from Ivete?

❓ Polite Requests
No hotel e no aeroporto, usamos expressões educadas para pedir informações ou permissão:
COULD YOU...?
"Could you spell your name, please?"
COULD I...?
"Could I use your pen?"
MAY I...?
"May I see your passport?"
