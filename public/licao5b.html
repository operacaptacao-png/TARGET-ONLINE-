💡 Grammar Tip
📚 Simple Present & Frequency Adverbs
Use the Simple Present for daily routines and schedules:

• Affirmative (+):
I / You / We / They start work at 8:30.
He / She / It starts work at 8:30. (add -s / -es)
He has lunch at 1:00 PM.

• Questions (?):
What time do you start work?
When does he have lunch?

• Frequency Adverbs:
always (100%), usually (80%), sometimes (50%), never (0%)
Place adverb before the main verb:
I always wake up early.
(Exception: place after verb to be: He is never late).
