💡 Grammar Tip

Present Simple describes routines:

I/you/we/they work; he/she/it works.

Questions use do/does: What time do you work? When does she start?

Prepositions of time: Use 'on' for days (on Monday), 'at' for clock times (at 9:00), and 'in' for parts of the day (in the morning).
