Language Guide

You can use the sentences below to help you learn how to talk about buying clothes.

“What size are you looking for?”
This is how you ask about the size of the clothes you want. You can practice saying sizes like “Extra Large,” “Large,” “Medium,” and “Small.”

“What color would you like to buy?”
Use this question to talk about the color of the clothes you want. Try saying colors like “red,” “blue,” “black,” and “green.”

“What are you looking for?”
This question helps you find the type of clothing you want. Practice saying what you’re looking for, like “a blue skirt,” “a red blouse,” “a pair of red shoes,” or “a pair of jeans.”

Language at Work

We use looking for to say the things we want.
• What are you looking for? I am looking for a dress.
• What is she looking for? She is looking for a black coat.
• May I help you, madam and sir? Yes, We are looking for two hats.
