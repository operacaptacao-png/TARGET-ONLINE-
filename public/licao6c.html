Past Simple Formations:

Regular verbs take -ed in affirmative statements:
e.g. call ➔ called, talk ➔ talked.

In negatives and questions, use DID + base verb:
e.g. She didn't call. Did he call you?

Never combine did with was/were:
Say: Did you enjoy? (NOT: Were you enjoy?)
