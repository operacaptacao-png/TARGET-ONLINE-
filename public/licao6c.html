Past Simple (Was/Were & Regular Verbs)

1. Verbo TO BE no Passado:

• I / He / She / It ➔ WAS (negativa: WASN'T)
• You / We / They ➔ WERE (negativa: WEREN'T)
The dinner was wonderful. They were very happy.

2. Verbos Regulares no Passado:

Acrescente -ed ou -d ao verbo:
• order ➔ ordered
• invite ➔ invited
• walk ➔ walked
• talk ➔ talked

3. Perguntas no Passado:

• Com BE: Was the food good? / Were you happy?
• Com outros verbos (DID): Did you talk a lot? / Where did you walk?
