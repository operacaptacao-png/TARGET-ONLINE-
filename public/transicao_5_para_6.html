Unit 5 to Unit 6 | The Job Offer & The Invitation

The tension that had filled the 14th-floor boardroom dissipated into joyful relief. Ivete had completely dazzled Mr. Jenkins, Charles, and the executive review board during her interview. She had articulated her international marketing achievements with flawless clarity, demonstrated her mastery of multi-channel strategies, and outlined her punctuality, organized daily habits, and collaborative routine. When the CEO officially announced that the position was hers, the entire department burst into genuine applause.
The office atmosphere instantly turned into a celebration. Her newly introduced colleagues—Claire, Brian, Gemma, and Tom—gathered around her desk with warm handshakes, enthusiastic welcomes, and sincere congratulations. As the working day concluded and team members began heading toward the elevators, Alan, who had watched her succeed with quiet admiration from his front desk, took a deep breath, gathered his courage, and stepped forward with an earnest smile.
"Ivete conseguiu o emprego! Alan a convida para jantar para comemorar."
Looking into her eyes, Alan asked if she would like to celebrate her triumphant day over dinner at an authentic bistro in Covent Garden. Heart still racing from the excitement of her official appointment, Ivete smiled warmly and accepted without hesitation. The corporate challenge was won; now, an enchanting evening across the lights and flavors of London was about to begin.
