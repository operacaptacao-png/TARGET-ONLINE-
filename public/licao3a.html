Language Guide

WHAT ARE YOU LOOKING FOR?
We use LOOKING FOR to say the things we want.

• What are you looking for? I am looking for a dress.
• What is she looking for? She is looking for a black coat.
• May I help you, madam and sir? Yes, We are looking for two hats.

Shopping for Clothes Audio

SHOPPING FOR CLOTHES

SA (Sales Assistant) – Good afternoon. Can I help you?
C (Customer) – Good afternoon. Yes, please. I am looking for a dress for a job interview.
SA – What colour would you like?
C – I don’t know. What colours are there, please?
SA – There are different colours. There are black, blue, pink, white and brown dresses. What would you like?
C – The white dress, please.
SA – Here you are.
C – Can I try it on?
SA – Of course. The changing room is over there.
(Some time later)
C – It’s incredible! How much is it?
SA – It’s 55 pounds.
C – Can I pay by card or cash only?
SA – You can pay in cash or by card, too.
C – Here is my card.
SA – Thank you. Your pin number, please.
C – Here it is. Thank you.
SA – Thank you very much for shopping with us today.
C – Bye!

💡 Grammar Tip
❓ Looking for...
Usamos LOOKING FOR para expressar o que estamos a procurar (especialmente em lojas). A estrutura muda com o sujeito:
I (Eu) -> AM
"I am looking for a dress."
HE / SHE / IT (Ele/Ela) -> IS
"She is looking for a coat."
YOU / WE / THEY (Tu/Nós/Eles) -> ARE
"We are looking for two hats."
"What are you looking for?"
